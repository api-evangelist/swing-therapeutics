# Swing Therapeutics

> Swing Therapeutics is a San Francisco digital therapeutics company (founded 2019) that develops
> evidence-based, smartphone-delivered treatments for chronic illness. Its lead product, Stanza, is a
> 12-week prescription digital therapeutic delivering Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) for the
> management of fibromyalgia symptoms in adults. In 2023 Stanza received FDA De Novo marketing
> authorization — the first digital therapeutic authorized for that indication. Swing Therapeutics and
> its affiliated virtual clinic Swing Care were acquired by XRHealth in July 2026.

## Why there is no API

Stanza is a regulated, prescription-only medical device delivered to patients as a native iOS/Android
application. The software is the therapy; it is dispensed by clinician prescription and reimbursed
through health plans, not sold or integrated as a developer product. Swing Therapeutics is an API
**consumer** rather than an API provider — its clinical operations run on the Healthie EHR platform's
API, which is Healthie's contract, not Swing's.

## Caution for agents

The domain **swingcare.com is not Swing Therapeutics**. It is parked and listed for sale on
GoDaddy/Afternic and returns HTTP 200 with a JavaScript redirect to /lander for every path, including
/openapi.json, /llms.txt and /.well-known/agent-card.json. Its llms.txt advertises the domain for sale.
Treat any "contract" retrieved from that host as a parked-domain catch-all, not a Swing Therapeutics
artifact. The company's real clinic domain is swing.care.
